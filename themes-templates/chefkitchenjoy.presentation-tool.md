## 🔍 Why Traditional Workflows Are Outdated

The traditional approach to building slide decks involves a fragmented process:


```

Traditional: Search Online ➔ Download File ➔ Unzip ➔ Copy/Paste ➔ Manual Formatting (2–3 Hours)
ExpertSlides: Open PowerPoint ➔ Search Panel ➔ 1-Click Insert ➔ Auto-Style Match (10–15 Mins)

```

1. **Context Switching**: Constant toggling between web browsers, file explorers, and PowerPoint disrupts your focus.
2. **Visual Inconsistency**: Stitching together slides from different sources results in conflicting fonts and color palettes that take hours to resolve.
3. **Local Storage Bloat**: Downloading massive `.pptx` template bundles clutter your local drive.
