# SciFig Scientific Poster & Presentation Templates

This is the official file archive of every free scientific poster template
and presentation (PowerPoint) template published on **[scifig.ai](https://scifig.ai)**.
All **154 template files** live here — **[`poster-templates/`](poster-templates/)**
(144 files) and **[`presentation-templates/`](presentation-templates/)** (10 slide decks).

Every file is a real, editable `.pptx` — open it in PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides.

Have a request or found a bug? [Open an issue](https://github.com/scifig-ai/scientific-poster-and-presentation-templates/issues).
If these templates helped you, a ⭐ star is always appreciated.

placeholder, and a QR-code placeholder for your paper or lab site.

so you can see the layout before you type.

## Why come here vs. the website

Use **this repository** if you already know which size or scenario you
need. Clone it, or open a folder and download the exact `.pptx`.

Use **[scifig.ai's template pages](https://scifig.ai/scientific-poster-templates)**
first if you want to *see finished poster examples* before picking one.
The site renders every size and color, and links each template straight
into the SciFig AI figure generator.

Looking for **poster examples** — finished, filled-in posters, not blank
templates? That's what the site's example gallery is for.

## FAQ

**What size is the A0 poster template?**
A0 is 841×1189mm (33.1×46.8in), the international standard poster size.
Both orientations are in [`poster-templates/a0/`](poster-templates/a0/).

**Can I use these templates in Google Slides?**
Yes — every file is a standard `.pptx`. Upload it to Google Drive and open
with Google Slides, or open directly in PowerPoint/Keynote/LibreOffice.
On scifig.ai's template pages, every template also has a one-click
**"Open in Google Slides"** button.

**Are these templates free?**
Yes, free for academic, research, and personal use.
See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) — the only restriction is re-uploading a file as
your own product elsewhere.

**What's the difference between a poster and an e-poster template?**
Poster templates (`a0/`, `a1/`, `us-poster-sizes/`, `46x46-square/`) are
sized for physical printing. `e-poster/` files are sized for on-screen
display and aren't meant to be printed at full size.

**Do these templates include real data or figures?**
No — every chart is placeholder/sample data (Group A/B, Condition 1/2,
etc.), so you can see the layout.
Replace it with your own, or generate real figures with [SciFig AI](https://scifig.ai).

**Where can I see finished poster examples, not blank templates?**
[scifig.ai](https://scifig.ai) — the template pages show rendered example
posters for every size and color.

**Why do some color variants share a preview image in this repo?**
The `.webp` previews are one representative render per layout family, not
a unique photo per color. The `.pptx` files are the full 6-color set.
